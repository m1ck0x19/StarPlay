# StarPlay
Its a simple star shaped device that sits on your desk and plays music through your computer when you push a button
![FinishedSchematic](FinishedSchematic)

So, im finally done, I ended up making the PCB as a rectangle and im panning on designing a 3D printed enclosure for it, as soon as I do, ill upload the files over here, it features an ESP32-c3 supermini, that comes with bluetooth and wifi, The screen is 1.28 inch LCD TFT display with a simple 6mm tactile button, when you press the button it runs a shortcut to your computer that opens up Spotify or another music playing app.
